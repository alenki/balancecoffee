# Photo finds: Biblioteka, Pushkina 35, Nalchik

Every photo here was checked to be this café, collected October 2026.
Instagram posts were kept only when the embed named @bibliotekanalchik as the
author; Yandex Maps and 2GIS photos come from the listings whose phone and
OGRNIP match `src/data/cafe.ts`. Not used: @_biblioteka_coffee and
@hi_nalchik, which are other cafés, and the VK page, which has one post.

This folder sits outside `assets-src/`, so the image pipeline ignores it.
To put a photo on a menu card, crop it to 4:5, save it under
`assets-src/menu/` and set `image` on the item in both `menu.ts` and
`menu.en.ts`.
